Diptiman Mohanta

Project Associate · SPIRE Lab, Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore

Automatic speech recognition · low-resource Indian languages · signal representations

I work on automatic speech recognition for low-resource Indian languages at the SPIRE Laboratory, IISc. I came to speech through signal processing: earlier work on biomedical signals and radar sensing, including complex-valued networks for atrial fibrillation detection published in IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence, shaped a continuing focus on signal representations that let models learn from limited data.

Currently

Building ASR systems for Indian languages with limited transcribed speech at SPIRE Lab, IISc Bangalore - dataset construction, text normalization for Indic scripts, and evaluation of multilingual and monolingual modeling approaches.

Current research

Speech recognition for low-resource Indian languages

End-to-end ASR under scarce transcribed speech: multilingual and monolingual modeling, text normalization for Indic scripts, and language modeling at the character, BPE, and state-space level.
